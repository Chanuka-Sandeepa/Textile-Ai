Research progress

Project Timeline

Four major phases documented across the team's individual component reports and the common integrated report.

  1. 1

    Research Proposal & Scope

    Problem statement, objectives, research questions and project scope formally documented. Literature survey covering textile waste classification, sustainability indicators, pricing models and marketplace forecasting conducted and referenced.

    Documented Evidence: proposal draft, group research paper
  2. 2

    Component Research & Model Development

    Four individual research reports prepared — each covering component scope, dataset, methodology, model selection, training and evaluation. EfficientNet-B3, MobileNetV2, XGBoost and Random Forest models trained and evaluated against documented metrics.

    Documented Evidence: four individual thesis reports
  3. 3

    Prototype Implementation

    Model APIs, integration workflow, seller-facing React interface and shared field contracts designed and reported. Each component's output feeds the next: fabric classification → sustainability scoring → commercial estimation → marketplace forecasting.

    Prototype evidence reported Evidence: architecture and API design in reports
  4. 4

    Final Evaluation

    Component metrics and limitations reported in October 2026. Prototype performance documented with synthetic and development data. Real-market stakeholder validation, temporal accuracy testing and production-scale calibration remain as future research directions.

    Real-market validation pending Evidence: component metrics in individual reports
