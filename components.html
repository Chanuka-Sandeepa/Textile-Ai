Individual Research Components

Four coordinated AI contributions to one integrated textile-waste marketplace.

Integration workflow: Fabric and condition signals → sustainability indicators → reference price and buyer categories → marketplace forecasts and traceability.
Member 01 · IT22281814

Fabric Intelligence

Transforms an uploaded textile image into a supported fabric class, confidence value, whole-image Defect/No Defect result and application-level condition signals.

Models

EfficientNet-B3, 16-class classifier; MobileNetV2 pathway for binary defect analysis.

Input

224×224 RGB image after documented preprocessing.

Reported results

89.68% test accuracy for fabric classification and 90.87% defect accuracy.

Outputs: fabric type, confidence, defect status, health score, repairability and estimated remaining lifespan. These are preliminary decision-support outputs, not laboratory certification.

Member 02 · IT22215338

Sustainability Intelligence

Uses structured textile and condition attributes to estimate circularity, recyclability and environmental indicators for downstream interpretation.

Inputs

Fabric type, weight, condition, health/repairability, district, province and industry context.

Outputs

Circularity, recyclability, carbon-reduction, water-reduction, CO₂-saving, water-saving and sustainability-grade outputs.

Evidence boundary

Reported regression results are development results based on synthetic or rule-based data and require real-factor validation.

Reported metrics: circularity R² 0.9657; recyclability R² 0.9502; carbon reduction R² 0.9589; water reduction R² 0.9389; CO₂ saving R² 0.9887; water saving R² 0.9854.

Member 03 · IT22221278

Commercial Intelligence

Connects assessed textile attributes to commercial decision support and preserves a traceable lifecycle history.

AI pricing

XGBoost regression estimates a reference price, not a guaranteed transaction price.

Buyer recommendation

Random Forest and XGBoost rank compatible buyer categories such as recyclers, apparel producers, designers and furniture makers.

Traceability

Events can cover analysis, listing, offer, transaction, delivery, impact and rating with identifiers and timestamps.

Reported results: price MAE 12.24, RMSE 15.40, R² 0.9590; buyer matching accuracy 83.56%. These results are prototype evidence on the documented evaluation data.

Member 04 · IT22281678

Marketplace Predictive Analytics

Estimates how a prepared textile listing may perform in the marketplace and supports seller planning.

Prediction tasks

Listing success (Sold/Unsold), estimated days to sell, demand level and market trend (Growing/Stable/Declining).

Dataset

100,000 synthetic records and 36 variables covering textile, sustainability, commercial, market, seller, temporal and engagement features.

Reported results

Listing success accuracy 83.65%; sales-time R² 0.7610; demand accuracy 99.54%; market-trend accuracy 98.72%.

Evidence boundary: Synthetic-data metrics do not establish real Sri Lankan market accuracy. Future work requires real transactions, temporal validation, calibration and monitoring.

Shared integration principle

Each component owns a distinct question and passes structured outputs downstream. The common report recommends stable material/listing identifiers, model versions, timestamps, input validation, controlled errors and human review for uncertain decisions.
