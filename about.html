How the Components Fit Together

Each member owns one component. Together they form a sequential workflow that describes a textile material, estimates its sustainability and commercial value, and forecasts marketplace performance.

Sustainability IntelligenceWhat is its environmental impact?
Commercial IntelligenceWhat is it worth, and who wants it?
